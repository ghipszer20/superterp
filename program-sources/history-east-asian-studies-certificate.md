Additional Requirements
An overall GPA of 2.0 in the certificate is required for graduation.
Nine of the 12 hours of electives must be upper division (3xx-4xx level courses).
A maximum of three credit hours of special topics courses on East Asia will be allowed with the approval of the certificate coordinator.
No more than nine credits from any one department or from the student's major may be applied toward the certificate.
No more than nine credits of the courses applied toward the certificate may be transferred from other institutions.
Students are asked to work with the coordinator in ensuring that the electives maintain an intercollegiate and interdisciplinary focus (at least three disciplines are recommended), as well as a regional distribution to reflect the countries of East Asia. Ideally, students will select electives that, taken together, include China, Japan, and Korea.

## Department page (https://sllc.umd.edu/fields/east-asian/certificate)

Core requirements (24 credits total):
East Asian history: 6 credits. Complete HIST284 East Asian Civilization I and HIST285 East Asian Civilization II.
East Asian language study: 6 credits. Complete six credits of Chinese, Japanese, or Korean language coursework at any level: CHIN121/122 or any other upper-level Chinese course, JAPN101, or KORA101 and KORA102.
East Asian Studies electives: 12 credits. Select approved courses from CEAS lists issued each semester. Lower-level electives: 3 credits at the 100-200 level. Upper-level electives: 9 credits at the 300-400 level.
Department/program limit: 9 maximum. No more than 9 certificate credits may come from the same academic program or department.
Major overlap: 9 maximum. No more than 9 credits used for a student's major may also count toward the certificate.
Approved elective offerings vary by semester.
