Students must earn a "C-" or above in each course applied toward the certificate.
Students must have a minimum 2.0 cumulative grade point average across all courses used to satisfy the undergraduate certificate requirements.
No more than 9 credit hours applied towards a major may be counted for the certificate.
No more than 9 credit hours may be taken at institutions other than UMD.
For more information, or to apply, please call the African American and Africana Studies Department at 301-405-1158. The AAAS office is located in 1119 Taliaferro Hall.

## Department page (https://aaas.umd.edu/undergraduate/certificate-african-american-studies)

Total credits: 21.
Foundation courses (9 credits): AAAS100 Introduction to African American Studies; AAAS101 Public Policy and the Black Community; and AAAS200 African Civilization or AAAS202 Black Culture in the United States.
Upper-level courses (9 credits): Students will select 3 AAAS upper-level (300/400) courses of their choice.
Seminar (3 credits), chosen as students near 85-90+ credits: AAAS400 Directed Readings in African American and Africana Studies, or AAAS402 Classic Readings in African American and Africana Studies.
All certificate requirements must be taken under the regular grading method and must be completed with a grade of C- or higher. No course with a grade less than C- may be used to satisfy requirements for the certificate program, and an overall GPA of 2.0 is required for graduation.
