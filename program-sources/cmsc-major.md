## Degree Requirements for CS Major

Much of the knowledge at the early stage of the degree program is cumulative. To ensure incoming first-year and transfer students start with the appropriate courses, the department offers exemption exams for CMSC131, CMSC132, CMSC216, and CMSC250. Students who have had CS courses prior to starting at Maryland are encouraged to take our exemption exams.

### Required Lower Level Coursework

All students, regardless of specialization, are required to take the following lower level coursework:

Math Requirements
- MATH140 (4) Calculus I
- MATH141 (4) Calculus II
- STAT4XX (3) This course must have a prerequisite of MATH141; Cannot be crosslisted with CMSC
- MATH/STATXXX (3/4) This course must have a prerequisite of MATH141; Cannot be cross-listed with CMSC
- \* Data Science, Machine Learning, and Quantum Information students must take a MATH Linear Algebra course (e.g. MATH240, MATH341 [must complete MATH340 first], or MATH461)

- CMSC132 (4) Object-Oriented Programming II*
- CMSC216 (4) Introduction to Computer Systems*
- CMSC250 (4) Discrete Structures*
- CMSC330 (3) Organization of Programming Languages
- CMSC351 (3) Algorithms
- \* An exemption exam is available for this course.

### Upper Level Concentration

All students, regardless of specialization, must complete 12 credit hours of 300 - 400 level courses in one discipline outside of Computer Science with a cumulative GPA of 1.7 or higher in this coursework. No course that is in, or crosslisted as, CMSC may be counted in this requirement (e.g., AMSC460). Only 1 independent study or experiential learning course may be used. Students who are pursuing a minor or a double major/dual degree may use those credits in this area with the exception of a few majors/disciplines (e.g., Information Science). Please consult with your advisor to ensure the courses you plan to take will satisfy this requirement. For a detailed breakdown of accepted Upper-Level Concentration courses, see the "Upper Level Concentration" page (excerpted below).

### Limited Enrollment Program (LEP) Benchmarks

These benchmarks apply to new freshman (matriculated Fall 2019 and later), new transfer students, and internal transfers changing majors within the University. All students will undergo a benchmark review at 45 and 75 credits. Students in the major are required to complete the following courses:

45-Credit Benchmark Requirements
- Completion of CMSC 131 with a minimum grade of C-
- Completion of CMSC 132 with a minimum grade of C-
- Completion of MATH 140 with a minimum grade of C-
- A minimum grade point average of 2.0 in all courses taken at the University of Maryland and all other institutions

75-Credit Benchmark Requirements
- Completion of CMSC 330 with a minimum grade of C-
- Completion of CMSC 351 with a minimum grade of C-
- Completion of a STAT4XX course (prerequisite: MATH141) with a minimum grade of C- or MATH/AMSC/STAT course (prerequisite: MATH141) with a minimum grade of C-
- A minimum grade point average of 2.0 in all courses taken at the University of Maryland and all other institutions

### Degree Requirements (Tracks)

Students within the Computer Science major may choose to pursue our General Track or one of four specializations offered: Cybersecurity, Data Science, Machine Learning, Quantum Information. Students are not required to pursue a specialization.

Students, regardless of specialization, are required to fulfill their computer science upper level course requirements from at least 3 areas. The five areas are: Area 1: Systems, Area 2: Information Processing, Area 3: Software Engineering and Programming Languages, Area 4: Theory, Area 5: Numerical Analysis.

For tracks allowing CMSC electives, three 1-credit CMSC STIC courses are equivalent to one 3-credit 300-400 level elective course.

## Upper Level Concentration (excerpt)

The Upper Level Concentration (ULC) requirement is unique to the Computer Science department. All Computer Science majors must complete 12 credit hours of 300-400 level courses in one discipline outside of Computer Science.

- No CMSC courses, or courses cross-listed with CMSC courses (e.g., AMSC460), or courses "credit only granted for" with CMSC courses (e.g. STAT426) are eligible for the ULC.
- No courses used for the Computer Science major can be used for the ULC.
- Only up to 3 credits from an independent study or experiential learning course may be used. Only up to 6 transfer credits may be used.
- ULC courses must be completed with a cumulative GPA of 1.7 or higher.
- Not Eligible for ULC (whole disciplines): College Park Scholars, Computer Engineering, Data Science, Honors, Information Science, Quantum Science Engineering. (Information Science/INST courses are accepted only for students declared in two specific Shady Grove minors.)

Courses Ineligible for Upper Level Concentration (excerpt of the department's course-by-course table; explicitly captioned "not exhaustive; updated with new courses regularly" and "send the syllabus to your advisor for review"):
- AMSC: 460, 466 (cross-listed with CMSC460/CMSC466)
- DATA: all (Data Science Minor), including DATA320, DATA400
- ENEE: 350, 426, 436, 446, 447, 456, 457, 459L, 464
- HONR/HNUH: all
- INST: all, except two specific Shady Grove minors
- MATH: 440, 456 (cross-listed with CMSC456/ENEE456), 461, 475 (cross-listed with CMSC475)
- PHYS: 457 (cross-listed with CMSC457), 474, 476, 486
- STAT: 426 (credit only granted for CMSC320), 464; "The STAT4xx course used to fulfill the CS math requirements cannot also count towards the ULC."
- Plus AOSC, AREC, BIOE, BSCI, BMGT, ECON, ENGL, GEOG, GEOL, IMDM, PHIL, PHPE, PSYC and Robotics-minor-specific exclusions (see the live page for the full, regularly-updated list).

## Cybersecurity Degree Requirements (excerpt)

Required: CMSC414, CMSC456. Choose four from: CMSC411, CMSC412, CMSC417, CMSC430, CMSC431, CMSC433, CMSC451. Upper Level Elective: 3 credits from CMSC 300- or 400-level (not CMSC330, CMSC351). (The academic catalog's "choose four" table for this specialization omits CMSC431.)

## Data Science Degree Requirements (excerpt)

Required: MATH240 or MATH461 or MATH341, STAT400, CMSC320, CMSC422, CMSC424. Choose one from: CMSC420, CMSC421, CMSC423, CMSC425, CMSC426, CMSC427, CMSC470. Choose one from: CMSC451, CMSC454, CMSC460. Choose two from: CMSC411, CMSC412, CMSC414, CMSC417, CMSC430, CMSC431, CMSC433, CMSC434, CMSC435, CMSC471. (The academic catalog's "choose two" table for this specialization omits CMSC431 and CMSC471, and only lists MATH240 rather than MATH240/MATH461/MATH341.)

## Machine Learning Degree Requirements (excerpt)

Required: MATH240 or MATH461 or MATH341, CMSC320, CMSC421, CMSC422. Choose two from: CMSC426, CMSC460/AMSC460, CMSC466/AMSC466, MATH401, CMSC470, CMSC472, CMSC473, CMSC474. Upper Level Elective: 6 credits from CMSC 3XX or CMSC 4XX (not CMSC330, CMSC351).

## Quantum Information Degree Requirements (excerpt)

Required: CMSC457, PHYS467. Choose four 400-level courses from at least two areas (excluding Area 4: Theory), no more than three per area: same Areas 1-5 course lists as the General Track (Area 3 here also includes CMSC431). Upper Level Elective: 3 credits from CMSC 3XX or CMSC 4XX (not CMSC330, CMSC351).
