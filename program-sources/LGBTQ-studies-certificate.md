## Department page (https://wgss.umd.edu/academic-programs/undergraduate/LGBTQ-Certificate)

The certificate in LGBTQ studies is a 21-credit interdisciplinary course of study comprised of 15 required and six elective credits. Students will earn a total of 21 credits, distributed as indicated below.
No course with a grade less than "C-" may be used to satisfy the certificate and an overall GPA of 2.0 in the certificate is required for completion.
At least 12 credits must be at or above the 300 level.
No more than 9 credits can overlap with the student's major.
Required: LGBT200 Introduction to LGBT Studies (3 credits, introductory); LGBT488 Senior Seminar or LGBT386 Undergraduate LGBT Internship (3 credits, capstone).
Electives: 15 credits from across at least 3 different thematic areas: Arts, Technology and Cultural Production; Transnational Politics and Perspectives; Institutions, Politics, and/or Social Movements; Race, Ethnicity, and Class; Transgender Studies; Queer and/or Feminist Theory. (The page names the areas but not which courses belong to each.)
